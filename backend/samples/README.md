# Sample transcripts

`transcripts/qmsum_*.txt` are converted from the test split of
[QMSum](https://github.com/Yale-LILY/QMSum) (MIT licence), using `backend/scripts/import_qmsum.py`.
Filler markers such as `{vocalsound}` were removed; the words are otherwise unchanged.

QMSum draws on the AMI and ICSI meeting corpora and on Welsh and Canadian parliamentary
committee meetings. Cite the QMSum paper (Zhong et al., NAACL 2021) if you publish results from
these files.

These are for testing the pipeline. They have no action-item labels yet; those come in Stage 7.
